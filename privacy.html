Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Hermes Admin Assistant ("the app") is a personal assistant used only by its owner. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app accesses the owner's Gmail messages and labels, Google Calendar events, Google Drive files, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out tasks the owner asks for, such as summarizing email, drafting and sending replies, managing calendar events, and finding or editing documents. It is not used for advertising, and it is not sold.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Email, calendar and document content is processed on that computer and sent to the AI model provider the owner has chosen, only as needed to complete the owner's requests.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with anyone else, except the AI model provider as described above or where required by law.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
