Hermes Admin Assistant

Hermes Admin Assistant is a personal AI assistant run by one person for their own use. It runs on the owner's own computer and, with the owner's permission, reads and manages the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts to help with email triage, scheduling, reminders and documents.

It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign up.
